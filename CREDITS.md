# Credits

## Cutting Mat wallpaper

The look of this theme (a cutting mat grid over a slowly moving field of colour, and the words "Stay Focused.") is inspired by the wallpaper [Cutting Mat | Seyul](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818894106) by Seyul Saltz for Wallpaper Engine, in its Universe palette. That wallpaper in turn credits [ShaderGradient](https://shadergradient.co) for its shader and the "Cutting Mat Wallpaper Packs by Robert McCombe with Rad" as its inspiration.

The stills in `src/frames/` are renders of that wallpaper's noise shader in its Universe palette. `src/build.py` regrades them darker when it packs them into the theme. The shader and the wallpaper's own files are not part of this repository.

These stills, and the background pictures built from them inside `focus-mat.json`, are included with credit and are not relicensed. They will be removed on request.
